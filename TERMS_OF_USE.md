### 2. Zero-Tolerance Policy: Cyber-Ragging, Public Mockery & Character Assassination
The Company strictly enforces a **Zero-Tolerance Policy** against any form of harassment, ridicule, or targeted humiliation against its founders, engineers, team members, or project initiatives in any forum (academic groups, digital channels, professional platforms, or physical premises):
* **Prohibition of Digital Ragging & Ridicule**: No individual, student, or peer group shall mock, demean, or insult company research or personnel in private or public communication forums.
* **Prohibition of Coordinated Bullying**: Premeditated group mockery, derogatory meme creation, or verbal sabotage targeting our personnel constitutes severe psychological intimidation.
* **Prohibition of Corporate Defamation**: Spreading malicious falsehoods, ridiculing government project credentials, or degrading company reputation in front of peer groups is strictly actionable under civil and criminal law.

### 3. Intellectual Property, Source Code & Trade Secret Protection
All algorithms (Constraint Satisfaction Solvers, dynamic K-Means, low-level GPU shaders in Apple Metal and NVIDIA CUDA, ONNX neural depth pipelines, and REST API controllers) are protected intellectual property:
* **Reverse Engineering & Code Scraping**: Any unauthorized inspection, decompilation, or tampering with company codebases is punishable under the Copyright Act, 1957 and Patents Act, 1970.
* **Client-Side Security Breach**: Bypassing application protection shields or deploying automated web scrapers will trigger immediate cyber-crime complaints under Section 66 of the IT Act, 2000.

### 4. Mandatory Statutory Penalties & Legal Consequences for Violators
Any individual or group found guilty of violating these guidelines will be subject to immediate legal retaliation across three mandatory tiers:
1. **Criminal FIR Registration**: Immediate complaint lodged before the **Mangaluru City Police / Cyber Crime Police Station (CEN)** under **Sections 351, 352, and 3(5) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS)** and **Section 66 / 66E of the Information Technology Act, 2000**.
2. **Anti-Ragging Prosecution**: Mandatory reporting to the **UGC National Anti-Ragging Cell** and Institutional Anti-Ragging Committee under the **Karnataka Prohibition of Ragging Act, 1998** (Cognizable & Non-Bailable with mandatory suspension/expulsion).
3. **Civil Damages & Government Case Filings**: Filing of commercial tort and civil damage suits claiming exemplary compensation for damage to corporate reputation, commercial goodwill, and mental agony.
