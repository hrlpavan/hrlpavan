# Statutory Legal Consequences, Penalties & Liabilities for Infringers & Bad Actors
**EXHAUSTIVE LEGAL DOCTRINE ON CIVIL & CRIMINAL PROCEEDINGS**

---

### ⚠️ SUMMARY OF LEGAL TROUBLE INFRINGERS WILL FACE:
Any individual, organization, competitor, or academic peer who engages in **trademark infringement, software piracy, source code theft, corporate defamation, cyber-bullying, or academic cyber-ragging** against HRL International will face coordinated action across six (6) distinct statutory regimes:

---

### 1. Criminal Prosecution for Software Piracy & Copyright Theft
* **Indian Copyright Act, 1957 (Sections 63, 63A, 64)**:
  * **Penalty**: Imprisonment for a term not less than **6 months up to 3 years**, plus a fine of **₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000**.
  * **Police Powers**: Police officers (Sub-Inspector and above) possess statutory power to **seize without warrant** all infringing copies, hard drives, servers, and computing devices.
  * **Digital Circumvention (Section 65A)**: Bypassing technical protection measures is punishable with up to **2 years imprisonment**.

---

### 2. Criminal Prosecution for Trademark Counterfeiting & Passing Off
* **Trade Marks Act, 1999 (Sections 103, 104, 105)**:
  * Applying false trademarks or selling goods/services under counterfeit marks is a **cognizable criminal offense**.
  * **Penalty**: Imprisonment from **6 months to 3 years** and fine up to **₹2,00,000**.
  * **Search & Seizure (Section 115)**: Police can conduct raids and seize all counterfeit brand assets.

---

### 3. Cyber Crimes, Unauthorized Hacking & Data Scraping
* **Information Technology Act, 2000**:
  * **Section 43 & 66 (Computer Related Offenses)**: Unauthorized downloading, copying, or extracting code from company servers/APIs — Imprisonment up to **3 years** or fine up to **₹5,00,000**, plus civil damages under Section 43 up to **₹1 Crore**.
  * **Section 66C (Identity Theft)**: Impersonating the company, its founders, or creating fake brand accounts — Imprisonment up to **3 years** and fine up to **₹1,00,000**.
  * **Section 66E (Privacy Violation & Harassment)**: Transmitting offensive digital material with intent to cause distress.

---

### 4. Criminal Defamation & Group Cyber-Ragging
* **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS) / IPC**:
  * **Section 352 BNS (Sec 504 IPC)**: Intentional insult with intent to provoke breach of peace.
  * **Section 351 BNS (Sec 506 IPC)**: Criminal intimidation and threats.
  * **Section 356 BNS (Sec 499/500 IPC)**: Criminal defamation damaging corporate commercial standing — Imprisonment up to **2 years**.
  * **Section 3(5) BNS (Sec 34 IPC)**: Common intention liability making every individual in a group equally guilty of the full offense.
* **The Karnataka Prohibition of Ragging Act, 1998**:
  * Ragging in physical or digital academic groups is a **Cognizable & Non-Bailable offense** punishable with **mandatory 1-year imprisonment** and **immediate expulsion/suspension** from the academic institution.

---

### 5. Multi-Crore Commercial Tort & Civil Damages
* **Injunctions & Asset Freezes**: Immediate ex-parte interim injunctions (*Anton Piller orders*) restraining the infringer from using the software or brand.
* **Exemplary & Punitive Damages**: Civil suits filed before the District Court / Commercial Court claiming damages up to **₹1,00,00,000 (Rupees One Crore)** for losses to business goodwill, investor valuation disruption, and mental trauma.
* **Account of Profits**: Mandatory disgorgement of all revenues generated by the infringer through unauthorized use.

---

### 6. International Cross-Border Enforcement (WIPO & US Federal Courts)
* **WIPO UDRP Proceedings**: Immediate transfer or cancellation of infringing domain names registered under `.com`, `.org`, `.io`, etc.
* **US Federal Court Actions (Lanham Act & DMCA § 512(f))**: Filing federal lawsuits in the United States District Court for trademark dilution, false advertising, and bad-faith statutory violations.
