## 2. High-Security Operational Protocol (Zero-Leak Governance)

Because government registrations and US incorporation involve highly confidential statutory documents (Aadhaar, PAN, Passports, Digital Signature Certificates (DSC), Board Resolutions, and Tax IDs), both Pavan and Samruddhi must strictly adhere to the following **Security Standard**:

1. **Dual-Authorization Rule**: No institutional filing, grant submission, or corporate document can be finalized without both Pavan and Samruddhi reviewing the submission draft.
2. **Encrypted Document Vault**: Never share raw government identity proofs, unredacted bank account statements, or DSC PINs over insecure platforms (e.g., standard WhatsApp chats). All official PDF files must be kept in the local offline vault or encrypted company drives.
3. **Official Communications Only**: All government, incubator, and US attorney correspondence must originate strictly from the verified corporate organization address:
   `hrlinternationalprivatelimited@gmail.com` (or official `@hrlinternational.com` domain).
4. **Strict IP Assignment**: All software runtimes (RTX-LocalAI, OpenFX plugins, InvigiMatrix, OmniTransform AI) remain 100% the exclusive corporate property of HRL International.

### Step 1: Delaware C-Corporation Formation
* **State**: State of Delaware (Division of Corporations, Dover, DE).
* **Entity Name**: **HRL International, Inc.**
* **Why Delaware**: Delaware General Corporation Law (DGCL) and the Court of Chancery are the undisputed global gold standard for high-growth tech companies.
* **Authorized Stock**: 10,000,000 shares of Common Stock at par value **$0.0001 per share**.
* **Initial Founder Allocation**: 8,000,000 shares issued to Pavan Kumar Sadashiv (with a dedicated equity/option pool reserved for key executive appointments).
* **Automated Formation Channels**: Stripe Atlas, Firstbase.io, or Clerky (cost: ~$500, includes Delaware registered agent and state filing fees).

---

### Step 2: IRS US Employer Identification Number (EIN)
* **Authority**: US Internal Revenue Service (IRS).
* **Form**: Form SS-4 (*Application for Employer Identification Number*).
* **Purpose**: Essential for opening US corporate bank accounts, hiring US contractors, and processing global payments.

---

### Step 3: US Business Banking (Mercury / Brex)
* **Bank**: Mercury Bank (Evolve Bank & Trust / Choice Financial Group — FDIC Insured up to $5M).
* **Capabilities**:
  * Hold USD reserves without mandatory Indian rupee conversion losses.
  * Direct integration with Stripe US, Apple App Store, and international SaaS invoicing.
  * Zero monthly account maintenance fees.

---

### Step 4: IRS Section 83(b) Election (CRITICAL 30-DAY DEADLINE)
* **What it is**: A statutory election filed directly with the IRS within **30 days** of acquiring founder shares.
* **Why it matters**: Locks in taxes at the initial nominal stock value ($0.0001/share). If missed, founders face massive US income tax bills as the company's valuation scales.

---

### Step 5: International Tax Treaty Compliance (Form W-8BEN-E & DTAA)
* **What it is**: *Certificate of Status of Beneficial Owner for United States Tax Withholding*.
* **Treaty**: **US-India Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement (DTAA)**.
* **Impact**: Eliminates the mandatory 30% US backup withholding tax on software royalty and cross-border digital payments, reducing it to treaty rates (0% to 15%).

---

### Step 6: US Federal Contracting & SAM.gov Registration (Optional / Scale)
* **System**: System for Award Management (`SAM.gov`) & CAGE Code allocation.
* **Purpose**: Allows HRL International to bid on US Federal tech procurement, DARPA/NSF AI grants, and Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) defense/software grants.
