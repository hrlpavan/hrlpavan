### 4. SOFTEX COMPLIANCE & RBI PROCEDURES
Under RBI Master Directions on Export of Goods and Services, all Indian entities exporting software via internet/data communication links must declare their exports via STPI:

1. **Submission Window**: Monthly aggregation of export invoices and contracts filed through the STPI Unit Management System (`stpionline.stpi.in`).
2. **Certification**: STPI verifies and certifies the declared software value and transmits validated records directly to RBI’s **EDPMS** portal.
3. **Banking Reconciliation**: Authorized Dealer (AD Category-I) banks issue the **Electronic Bank Realization Certificate (e-BRC)** upon foreign inward remittance matching the certified Softex number, ensuring complete statutory immunity and zero foreign exchange regulatory friction.
