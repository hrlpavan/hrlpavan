## 2. The Genesis & Founding Vision

HRL International began with a fundamental premise: **the global software, visual computing, and creator tooling industry has long been monopolized by exorbitant foreign licensing fees and opaque cloud architectures.** A standard VFX plugin suite or professional-grade color engine often costs upwards of ₹30,000 (\$350+) per user, creating massive barriers for independent creators, universities, and boutique studios across India and emerging markets.

Recognizing this gap, technologist and software engineer **Pavan Kumar Sadashiv** set out to build an autonomous, first-principles software powerhouse from Mangaluru. The founding philosophy was simple: **engineer high-performance, hardware-accelerated software that runs locally with zero cloud lag, zero data-harvesting, and at an ultra-accessible price point.**

### Phase 2 — Deep-Tech GPU & OpenFX Engineering (Late 2025 – Early 2026)

HRL transitioned from creator production to systems software engineering:

- **DaVinci Resolve OpenFX (OFX) Runtimes**: Studio-grade plugins natively targeting DaVinci Resolve 20/21 and Adobe ecosystems using C++, Metal (Apple Silicon), and CUDA (NVIDIA).
- **Hollywood ACEScc Color Science**: Integrated authentic 12-Bit DPX RAW sensor flow and ACES color pipelines for indie creators without five-figure licensing overhead.
- **The Zero-Cloud Privacy Guarantee**: All neural frame passes, depth maps, and shaders process **100% locally on the user's GPU** — 0 ms cloud latency, zero telemetry scraping.

### Phase 3 — Algorithmic Systems & Institutional Solvers (2026)

HRL expanded into high-stakes algorithmic optimization and institutional software:

- **InvigiMatrix (Exam Automation Engine)**: Automated institutional exam seating and invigilation scheduling platform solving complex **NP-Hard Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSP)**. Reduces university exam hall allocation from days to sub-second automated runs.
- **LocalAI & Neural Speech Runtime**: Sub-75ms conversational Voice AI and low-latency local inference engines delivering **1,538+ tokens/sec** on consumer hardware.
- **HRL-Lang Compiler Core**: Custom LLM-friendly language runtime with zero memory fragmentation guarantees.
