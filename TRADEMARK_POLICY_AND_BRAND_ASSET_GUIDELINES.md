### 3. Permitted vs. Prohibited Brand Usage

#### ✅ Permitted Usage (Nominal Fair Use):
* Mentioning HRL International in news articles, research papers, or academic reviews accurately referring to the company or its products.
* Demonstrating tutorial workflows using authentic HRL plugins while providing explicit attribution: *"Rendered using AI Cinematic Haze™ by HRL International Private Limited"*.

#### ❌ Strictly Prohibited Usage:
* Using `HRL`, `HRL International`, or any confusingly similar mark as part of your company name, domain name, social media handle, or product title.
* Modifying, distorting, or altering the colors, typography, or proportions of the official HRL wordmarks or logos.
* Displaying HRL trademarks in a misleading context that implies endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation without written authorization from the Board of Directors.
* Creating counterfeit plugins, cracked distributions, or repacked software installers bearing the HRL brand mark.
